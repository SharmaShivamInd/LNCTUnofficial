Why this exists

Before my first mid-sem, I wanted to know what kind of questions actually get asked — one focused night beats a week of guessing. The catch: only one PYQ was floating around.

This site fixes that by collecting papers subject-wise for every semester, so you always know what to expect walking into an exam hall.
